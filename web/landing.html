Three steps from a long video to something you can post.

Paste a link

A YouTube, Twitch or Kick video or stream, or upload a file. Add a streamer once and Muzzcut can watch their channel for you.

Muzzcut picks the moments

It transcribes the audio on your own machine, then asks your AI which moments would hold a stranger's attention. No AI key? It picks by loudness.

Post the clips

Each clip is cut to 9:16 with word-by-word captions and a hook on top. Download it, or queue it to YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.

Pick how the words land.

Seven caption styles. These five are the ones people reach for first. Every clip also gets a subtitle file and an optional @handle watermark.

What it does besides cutting.

Clips a stream while it is live

Records into a rolling buffer and clips highlights as they happen, using loudness, chat activity and the transcript.

Works with your own AI

Gemini, OpenAI, Grok, Groq, OpenRouter, Claude, a local model through Ollama, or anything that speaks the OpenAI format.

Hindi and Urdu in Roman letters

Speech in Hindi or Urdu is captioned in Roman letters, so the captions read the way people type it.

A workspace for each creator

Their own channels, clip settings, posting rules and activity log, so one install can look after many streamers.

Posts for you

Connect YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, then queue clips or auto-post with spacing and daily limits.

Subtitles and watermark

Every clip comes with an .srt file, and your @handle can sit near the top of each video.

It runs on your computer, with your key.

Questions people ask first.

Does it cost anything?

Muzzcut is free. You pay the AI service you pick for what you use. A one-hour video takes about four requests.

Can I use it without an AI key?

Yes. Clips are then picked by loudness instead of by what is said, so results are rougher.

Which links and files work?

YouTube, Twitch and Kick links, or a video file you upload.

Where do my videos go?

Into the data folder on the computer or server that runs Muzzcut. Nothing is uploaded to a Muzzcut service.

Do I need permission to clip a video?

Clip videos you made or have permission to use. You are responsible for the rights to what you download and post, and for following the rules of the sites involved.
